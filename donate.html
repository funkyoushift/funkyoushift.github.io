The Hunt / charity events

Community hunts, giveaways, and St. Jude PLAY LIVE

The Hunt is a community event where players chase goals, complete challenges, watch streams, and keep up with giveaways. When we tie an event into charity, donations should go through the official St. Jude PLAY LIVE campaign or the fundraiser link posted in Discord.

What is the Hunt?A community challenge/event built around Borderlands, streams, giveaways, and Discord updates.
How do I join?Join Discord for rules, live updates, announcements, and giveaway details.
How does charity work?Charity pushes are handled through St. Jude PLAY LIVE or official fundraiser links so donations go directly where they should.
