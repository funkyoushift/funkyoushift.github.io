Quick answers

How do I get Borderlands modded weapons?

The fastest route is to join the Discord, follow the server rules, and ask in the correct modded gear or trading area.

Are modded weapons the same as normal trading?

Not always. Normal trading is usually in-game loot. Modded gear can involve edited or custom items, so ask in the right place.

Can I ask if I do not know the exact item name?

Yes. Give as much detail as you can: game, platform, character, item type, screenshot if possible, and what you want it to do.
