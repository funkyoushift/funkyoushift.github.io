Add a loadout

Paste your Borderlands 4 item codes or upload a collection. Preview it here, then send it for approval. No app or account required.

Your loadout

Maximum 16 MB. Exact code case, item order, duplicates, and JSON subfolders are preserved. JSON can be a library download or a version 1 folder.

Review your loadout

Add a screenshot beside any item. PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 12 MB each; images are resized for the website. Up to 20 screenshots per loadout. If none is uploaded, we look for a matching GZO screenshot or a stored game card using the exact item code.

My submissions on this browser

Check approval status, retry an interrupted upload, or withdraw your submission. Keep this browser's site data to retain access. Clearing it removes your ability to manage these submissions.

Approval checks a community submission; it does not prove that gear is legitimate or safe to equip.
