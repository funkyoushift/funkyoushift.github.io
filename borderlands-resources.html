Quick answers

Are these tools made by FunkYouSHiFT?

No. These are third-party and community resources. Use their instructions, follow their rules, and ask in Discord if you need help understanding what you are looking at.

Can trainers or save editors get me in trouble?

They can create problems if you use them carelessly. Keep trainer use to solo/offline or private play, scan downloads, and do not use tools to ruin public games.

Why are some resources buttons instead of embeds?

Some tools block iframe embeds or are too heavy for mobile. Buttons keep the page fast and avoid broken boxes.

Where do I go if I still need help?

Join the Discord and ask. Someone can usually point you in the right direction faster than guessing alone.
