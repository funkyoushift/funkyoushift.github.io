Quick answers

What are these Borderlands resources for?

They are quick links and embeds for build videos, item lookup tools, and outside resources that help players find answers faster.

Why do some embeds look like previews?

Some outside sites block full embedded pages, so the page includes fallback buttons that open the tools directly.

Are these official game resources?

No. They are useful third-party/community resources linked from the FunkYouSHiFT hub.
