Item codes, save editors, and modding tools

A gear drop and an item code are different ways to get started. A code must be imported with a compatible tool; copying one does not put an item in your inventory.

The Borderlands 4 community library contains shared item codes and collections. For editing your own saves, visit save editors and Borderlands tools. The modding guide explains the different options.

Playing on PC, PlayStation, or Xbox?

Include your platform when requesting gear. Do not assume a PC save editor works on a console, or that a specific item transfers across every platform and game update. Ask about the current lobby's compatibility before joining.

Using modded gear

Back up saves where your platform supports it. Understand what a mod changes: modded sessions may affect more than your weapons. Respect other players and avoid bringing modded gear into their game without permission.
