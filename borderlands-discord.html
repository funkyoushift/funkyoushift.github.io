Quick answers

Is this for BL3 and BL4?

Yes. The community covers Borderlands players, trading, modded gear, builds, and discussion as new content changes.

Can I ask for modded weapons?

Yes, but read the rules first and ask in the right place. Include your game, platform, and what you need.

Is GenieBot yours?

No. GenieBot and GZO are third-party. Respect their rules and use their support channels if you need bot help.
